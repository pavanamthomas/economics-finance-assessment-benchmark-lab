# Difficulty calibration

Used when writing and when arguing with a tag.

**MEDIUM.** One mechanism, directly applied. Little branching. Examples: Roll bounce with a frozen book (`MM-M-01`); LCR versus NSFR on one balance sheet (`MP-M-02`); \(R'(d)=-t/q\) (`UE-M-01`); CPAMM spot \(y/x\) (`TD-M-01`).

**HARD.** Combine two objects, or run a comparative static, or keep a realistic misconception off the key. Examples: Kyle \(\lambda\) versus \(\beta\) versus \(\sigma_v/\sigma_u\) (`MM-H-01`); CCyB timing plus “gap is only a guide” (`MP-H-01`); fourfold pattern not equal to \(\lambda\) (`BF-H-01`); open versus closed city (`UE-H-01`); IL formula plus hold counterfactual (`TD-H-01`).

**EXPERT.** The construct is an assumption, an information set, an identification split, or a non-obvious boundary. Algebra length is not the criterion. Examples: nested executable books (`MM-E-01`); Nash fire sale versus freeze (`MP-E-01`); reference point recodes CPT (`BF-E-01`); Roback \((w,R)\) cells (`UE-E-02`); three AMM prices and a rejected unnamed-“price” draft (`TD-E-01`).

A glossary card keyed correctly and labelled Expert is `RJ-06`.
